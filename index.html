About

Background and interests

I completed my B.Sc. in Civil Engineering at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST), Dhaka, in July 2026 with a CGPA of 3.907 out of 4.00, securing the first position in the Spring 2025 semester. My undergraduate thesis, supervised by Prof. Dr. Md. Shahid Mamun, compared machine learning models for predicting fatal and non-fatal road crash severity, with an emphasis on interpreting why a model reaches a given prediction rather than accuracy alone.

Alongside that, my project work covers traffic microsimulation of a major Dhaka intersection in PTV VISSIM and construction detailing. My continuing interest is measurement: getting reliable traffic and safety data out of a mixed-traffic city, and modelling it in a way practising engineers can still interrogate.

Outside coursework I served as Vice President and General Secretary of the AUST Civil Engineering Society (ACES) and held several executive roles in the ACI AUST Student Chapter.
